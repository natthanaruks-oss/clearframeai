# Business Hypothesis — ClearFrame AI

All prices and market positions below are hypotheses for validation, not verified market facts.

## Positioning

**“Make unclear images usable again — while keeping the original as the source of truth.”**

The product should not compete only on “4x upscale.” The differentiators should be:

1. Simple Thai-first workflow
2. Truth-conscious Natural mode
3. Private, no-history option
4. Business batch workflow later
5. Clear distinction between ordinary enhancement and generative restoration

## Initial Beachhead

Start with business users who receive poor-quality operational images:

- Sales field photos
- Site progress photos
- Product / inventory images
- Customer-submitted images
- Scanned or photographed documents

This segment has a repeatable pain point and may value batch processing, standard output and privacy more than casual consumers.

## Monetization Experiments

### Experiment A — Credit Pack

- Test range: THB 39–99 per pack
- Purpose: validate willingness to pay without subscription resistance

### Experiment B — Individual Pro

- Test range: THB 99–199/month
- Candidate benefits: higher limits, 4x, batch, no watermark

### Experiment C — Business Workspace

- Test range: THB 990–2,990/month
- Candidate benefits: batch upload, team presets, API, usage report, private processing policy

Do not finalize pricing before measuring actual cost per successful image and repeat usage.

## Core Metrics

- Upload → Enhance conversion
- Enhancement success rate
- Download rate
- Repeat user rate
- Cost per successful enhancement
- Average latency
- Percentage selecting Maximum
- Refund / complaint rate from altered details
- B2B batch demand

## Commercial Risk

The market contains many generic upscalers. A weak product would be easy to copy. Long-term defensibility should come from:

- Workflow integration
- Thai business use cases
- Controlled presets and governance
- Customer-specific batch rules
- Quality benchmark data
- API and enterprise privacy options
